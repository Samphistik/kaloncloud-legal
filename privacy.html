Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who we are

Kal On Cloud ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted publishing tool operated by a single individual for their own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects an account (YouTube, TikTok or Instagram), the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the account name) through the platform's official OAuth login. The app uses this only to upload the owner's own videos and images and to read the owner's own channel statistics.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and content are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data about other users or viewers.

Data retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect any account at any time from the app, which deletes the stored token. Access can also be revoked in each platform's security settings (for example TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites).

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above will change when it is.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted as an issue in this site's GitHub repository.
